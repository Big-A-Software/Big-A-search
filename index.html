Search, built differently.

Lux is a free and open-source search engine developed by Big-A. It searches a selected and growing collection of websites while Big-A develops the crawler, index, ranking, and developer tools behind it.

About Lux

Lux is a free and open-source search engine developed by Big-A.

Lux is one of the projects developed by Big-A.

Rather than attempting to search every website on the Internet, Lux focuses on a selected and growing collection of websites.

Its source code is designed to remain straightforward and understandable so people can examine, modify, learn from, and contribute to the project.
